Partial Payments Are Accepted

Yes, Brightline Solar does accept partial payments on outstanding invoices. You can submit any amount greater than $10.00 toward a balance due without the payment being rejected. However, a partial payment does not satisfy the invoice in full, and the remaining balance will continue to accrue a 1.5% monthly late fee (applied on the 10th calendar day after the original due date) until the balance is cleared.

How to Submit a Partial Payment in Brightline Home

  1. Open the Brightline Home app and tap Billing in the bottom navigation bar.
  2. Select the invoice you want to pay, then tap Make a Payment.
  3. On the payment screen, tap Enter a custom amount and type the amount you wish to pay.
  4. Choose your payment method and tap Submit Payment.

You will receive an email and in-app receipt within 15 minutes confirming the amount applied. The invoice detail screen will update to show the new remaining balance, marked as Partially Paid.

Will a Partial Payment Prevent Account Suspension?

A partial payment reduces your balance but does not automatically pause suspension proceedings. If your account is already in a past-due state, contact Brightline Support at 1-800-555-0192 or chat in-app to set up a payment arrangement. Customers facing financial hardship may also qualify for deferred billing through our assistance program.
