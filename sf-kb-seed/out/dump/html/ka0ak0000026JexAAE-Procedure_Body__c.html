Before you begin: Brightline will send an inspection confirmation notification in the Brightline Home app (under My Project > Permits > Upcoming Inspections) at least 48 hours before your scheduled date. Your inspection window is typically 4 hours (e.g., 8 a.m.–12 p.m.). An adult 18 or older must be present for the full window. If you need to reschedule, do so at least 24 hours in advance to avoid a $75 rescheduling fee charged to your account.

  1. Open Brightline Home, tap My Project, then tap Permits to confirm the inspection date, time window, and the name of the assigned municipal inspector.
  2. Clear a 3-foot path from your front door to your main electrical panel. Remove vehicles, storage bins, or equipment blocking the panel door or the area beneath your solar array.
  3. Locate and set aside any documentation the inspector may request: your Brightline installation completion certificate (available under My Project > Documents), the permit placard, and your interconnection approval letter.
  4. Ensure your Brightline gateway device has a solid green LED. If it shows amber or red, restart it by unplugging it for 30 seconds and reconnecting. A green light confirms the monitoring system is online, which the inspector will verify remotely.
  5. If you have a battery storage unit, leave it in Auto mode—do not manually force it to charge or discharge on inspection day, as the inspector will test automatic transfer switch behavior.
  6. On inspection day, greet the inspector at the scheduled start of your window and guide them to the electrical panel, attic access (if applicable), and roof access point.
  7. After the inspection, check My Project > Permits within 2 business days for your pass/fail result and next steps toward interconnection.

Troubleshooting

Inspector did not arrive within my window. Wait until 30 minutes past the end of your window, then call Brightline Support at 1-800-555-2477. Do not leave the property before calling; departing early is recorded as a missed inspection and may incur the $75 rescheduling fee.

Inspection failed due to a wiring discrepancy. Brightline will contact you within 1 business day to schedule a remediation visit at no charge if the issue originated from our installation. A re-inspection will then be booked automatically.

Gateway shows amber on inspection day. Follow the steps in How Do I Troubleshoot a Microinverter That Will Not Come Back Online for connectivity guidance, or call Support so a technician can push a remote reset before the inspector arrives.

For rescheduling after a weather cancellation, see How to Reschedule Your Final Inspection After a Weather Delay.
