Before you begin: You must have a confirmed service appointment on your Brightline Home account. You will receive a confirmation email with your appointment ID (format: SVC-XXXXXXXX) at least 48 hours before your visit. Ensure any roof-mounted obstructions such as satellite dishes or temporary equipment are removed before the crew arrives. If you live in a gated community or building with secured roof access, read Step 4 carefully.

  1. Open the Brightline Home app and tap Service in the bottom navigation bar.
  2. Tap Upcoming Appointments and select the relevant appointment by its date or appointment ID.
  3. Tap Access & Preparation to open the roof access checklist.
  4. Toggle Roof Access Confirmed to On. If access requires a gate code, key, or building manager contact, tap Add Access Notes and enter the details. These notes are sent securely to your assigned crew lead at least 2 hours before the arrival window opens.
  5. If a dog or other animal has access to the yard or roof area, tap Pet on Property and follow the on-screen guidance to note containment arrangements.
  6. Tap Save & Confirm. You will see a green banner reading Access details saved and receive a confirmation SMS.

Troubleshooting

The Access & Preparation section is greyed out. This usually means your appointment is within 1 hour of the arrival window, at which point access notes are locked. Call Brightline Scheduling at 1-800-555-0192 to relay access information directly to dispatch.

I saved my access notes but the crew said they never received them. Notes sync to the crew app within 15 minutes of saving. If your appointment is more than 2 hours away, tap Access & Preparation again and verify the toggle is still On. If the toggle reset, this may indicate a sync error; force-close and reopen the app, then re-save. For further help, see What is a crew arrival window and how long is it?

My appointment does not appear under Upcoming Appointments. Appointments may take up to 30 minutes to appear after scheduling. If the appointment still does not show, contact support with your appointment ID.
