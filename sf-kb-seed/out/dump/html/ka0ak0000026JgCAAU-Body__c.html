Background

Prior to this release, the Brightline monitoring platform issued a BATT_OFFLINE_001 alert after just 90 seconds of missed telemetry from a battery unit. Network micro-outages—common during router reboots or brief cellular signal drops—routinely triggered this alert even when the battery itself was fully operational.

What Is Changing

  • Effective date: August 5, 2026
  • New confirmation window: 4 minutes of sustained offline status before BATT_OFFLINE_001 is issued.
  • Auto-clear: If telemetry resumes before the alert is sent, no notification is generated and no manual dismissal is needed.
  • Persistent offline: If the battery remains offline beyond 4 minutes, the alert fires as normal and a follow-up BATT_OFFLINE_002 escalation triggers after 2 hours of continued silence.

No Action Required

This improvement is applied automatically to all accounts. You do not need to update the app or change any settings. To learn how to configure suppression rules for other alert types, visit how to configure false alarm suppression rules in Brightline Home.
