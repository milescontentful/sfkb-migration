Effective date: February 15, 2026

Accounts that reach the end of the 14-day retry window (PAY-4010) for a second consecutive billing cycle will enter the following escalation path:

StageTriggerAccount Impact
Stage 12nd consecutive failed cycleRead-only mode in Brightline Home; battery scheduling disabled.
Stage 230 days after Stage 1 with no paymentProduction monitoring access suspended; error ACC-5030 displayed.
Stage 360 days after Stage 1 with no paymentAccount sent to collections; reinstatement fee of $75 required.

To restore account standing at any stage, navigate in Brightline Home to Account → Billing → Resolve Balance and complete payment of all overdue amounts plus any assessed fees.

Customers who believe a suspension was applied in error should first confirm their payment history, then open a billing dispute through Account → Billing → Dispute a Charge. For full dispute guidance, see How Do I Dispute a Charge on My Brightline Invoice.

Hardship deferral requests must be submitted before Stage 2 to remain eligible.
