What Changed

Effective March 10, 2026, the following lockout thresholds apply to all Brightline Home accounts:

Failed AttemptsLockout DurationRecovery Method
3 consecutive15 minutes (automatic)Wait and retry
6 consecutive2 hours (automatic)Wait and retry or email verification
10 consecutiveIndefiniteSupport contact required

How to Recover a Locked Account

  • Open Brightline Home and tap Trouble signing in? on the login screen.
  • Select Unlock My Account and complete the email verification step.
  • For indefinite lockouts, call Brightline Support at 1-800-555-0194 (Mon–Fri, 7 AM–8 PM local time).

Partner API integrations authenticating on behalf of customers should review how to resolve OAuth2 token errors in the Partner API to handle lockout-related token rejections gracefully. No fees are associated with account recovery.
