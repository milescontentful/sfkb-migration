Entering a Custom Payment Amount

  1. Open Brightline Home and tap Billing in the bottom navigation bar.
  2. Tap Make a Payment, then select your preferred payment method (bank account, debit card, or credit card).
  3. On the amount screen, tap Enter Custom Amount instead of using the pre-filled statement balance.
  4. Type your desired payment amount — the minimum accepted partial payment is $10.00.
  5. Review the summary screen and tap Confirm Payment. You will receive an email and in-app confirmation within a few minutes.

Payment-Application Order

When a partial payment is received, Brightline applies funds in the following priority order:

PriorityCharge Type
1Outstanding late fees
2Previous-cycle carried-forward balance
3Current monitoring and service plan charges
4Current energy or lease charges

This order ensures that the oldest and highest-priority obligations are cleared first, reducing the risk of additional fees accruing.

Confirming How Your Payment Was Applied

After your payment posts (typically within 1–2 business days), go to Billing > Payment History and tap the transaction to see a breakdown showing exactly how each dollar was allocated.

If you believe a partial payment was applied incorrectly, see How Do I Dispute a Charge on My Brightline Invoice. If you are facing ongoing financial difficulty, learn about deferred-payment options at Hardship Program Expansion: Extended Deferral.
