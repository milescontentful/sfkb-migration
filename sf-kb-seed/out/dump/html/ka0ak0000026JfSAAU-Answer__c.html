Finding and Downloading Past Receipts

Brightline Home stores receipts for every successful payment for up to 7 years, and you can download a PDF copy at any time directly from the app or web portal.

Steps to locate a past receipt

  1. Log in to Brightline Home (app or browser at home.brightlinesolar.com).
  2. Navigate to Account → Billing & Payments → Payment History.
  3. Use the date range filter to narrow results, or enter a payment confirmation number (e.g., BL-RCT-XXXXXXXX) in the search bar.
  4. Tap the payment row to open the detail view.
  5. Tap Download Receipt (PDF). The file will save to your device or open in a new browser tab.

If a receipt is missing

Receipts only exist for successful payments. If a payment failed or is still pending, no receipt will appear. You can also request a receipt be re-sent to your email by tapping Resend Receipt on the detail view — delivery takes up to 10 minutes.

For guidance on how to get receipts sent to you automatically going forward, see How do I get a receipt for my Brightline payment?
