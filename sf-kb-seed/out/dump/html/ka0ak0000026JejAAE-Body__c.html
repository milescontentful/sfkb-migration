Released: April 14, 2026

Temperature extremes are one of the leading causes of temporary solar and battery output loss. The new temperature warning alert system continuously monitors thermal data reported by your inverter and battery management unit and notifies you the moment readings enter a caution or critical range.

Alert Thresholds at Launch

  • Panel surface – Caution: ≥ 175°F or ≤ 0°F
  • Panel surface – Critical: ≥ 185°F or ≤ −10°F
  • Battery module – Caution: ≥ 105°F or ≤ 23°F
  • Battery module – Critical: ≥ 113°F or ≤ 14°F

Alerts are delivered via push notification, email, and in-app banner. You can adjust notification channels anytime under Settings → Alerts → Temperature Warnings in Brightline Home.

Critical alerts include a one-tap shortcut to your battery control panel so you can switch storage mode to Protect, which reduces charge/discharge cycling during thermal stress.

If you are seeing an unusually high volume of alerts, review our guidance on how Brightline reduces false alarm rates before contacting support.

Update to app version 4.7.0 via your device's app store to enable this feature. No additional hardware is required.
