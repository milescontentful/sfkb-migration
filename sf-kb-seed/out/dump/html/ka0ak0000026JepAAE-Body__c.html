As of March 3, 2026, weather adjustment data is automatically included in every production report accessed via Reports > Production > Monthly Summary or Reports > Production > Annual Overview in Brightline Home.

What's New

  • Weather Adjustment Factor (WAF): A percentage displayed alongside each period's actual output, showing how much weather reduced or exceeded the baseline irradiance model.
  • Adjusted Estimate column: Your original production estimate revised by the WAF for direct comparison.
  • Weather Event Flags: Periods affected by named weather events (e.g., storm systems, extended overcast) are tagged with a W indicator.
