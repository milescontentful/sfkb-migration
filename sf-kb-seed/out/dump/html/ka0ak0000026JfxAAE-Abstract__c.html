Starting April 14, 2026, all Brightline Solar residential customers whose systems have passed final inspection can book their post-install walkthrough directly through the Brightline Home app under My System > Appointments > Schedule Walkthrough. The self-service tool shows real-time availability for your assigned field technician and sends an automated confirmation with a calendar invite within five minutes of booking. Walkthroughs must be completed within 21 calendar days of your Permission to Operate (PTO) date; failure to schedule within that window will generate an in-app alert and may delay monitoring activation.
