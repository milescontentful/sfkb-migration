Background

Until August 31, 2026, customers requesting a payoff letter through Brightline Support faced two tiers: a free 5-business-day option and a $25 expedited 1-business-day option. With the launch of the self-serve payoff letter tool in Brightline Home, demand for phone-assisted requests has shifted primarily to complex loan scenarios (e.g., co-borrower situations, refinance payoffs). To simplify the process, Brightline is standardizing support-assisted requests to a single, free 1-business-day SLA.
