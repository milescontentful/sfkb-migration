Before you begin: You must be logged in to the Brightline Home app (version 4.2.0 or later) as the account owner or an authorized household member. Lifetime stats are available starting 24 hours after your system's Permission to Operate (PTO) date is recorded in your account. If your system was installed before March 2023, data is displayed from the earliest available meter reading, which may not represent day one of operation.

  1. Open the Brightline Home app and tap Dashboard on the bottom navigation bar.
  2. Tap the Reports tab at the top of the screen.
  3. Select Production Summary from the report type menu.
  4. Tap the Date Range picker and choose Lifetime from the dropdown. The picker defaults to the current calendar month.
  5. Review the summary cards that appear: Total kWh Generated, Estimated CO₂ Offset (lbs), and Estimated Bill Savings ($). Savings figures are calculated using your rate plan on file and are estimates only.
  6. Tap View Detailed Breakdown to see a year-by-year table of annual production totals.
  7. To save the data, tap the Share icon (upper right) and choose Download PDF or Export CSV. The file will be emailed to your account email address within five minutes.

Troubleshooting

Lifetime option is grayed out: Your system's PTO date may not yet be recorded. Contact Brightline Support if it has been more than 48 hours since your system was activated.

Totals appear lower than expected: If you had a monitoring gateway replacement or a microinverter swap, a small data gap may exist. See What causes a microinverter to show offline in Brightline Home for context on data gaps caused by offline events.

Export email never arrives: Check your spam folder. If it does not arrive within 15 minutes, retry the export or review your account email address under Settings > Account Info. For help updating your email, see How do I change my email address in Brightline Home.
