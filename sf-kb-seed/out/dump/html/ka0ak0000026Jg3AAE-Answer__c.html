Co-Borrower Rights

A co-borrower on a Brightline solar loan is a full legal party to the loan agreement — not just a cosigner. This means they have independent rights to:

  • Log in to Brightline Home with their own credentials and view the full loan dashboard, including statements and payment history
  • Make payments, set up AutoPay, and download receipts
  • Request a payoff letter through Account > Financing > Request Payoff Letter
  • Contact Brightline support to discuss account details without needing authorization from the primary borrower

Co-Borrower Responsibilities

Co-borrowers share joint and several liability for the entire loan balance. This means:

  • Both parties are equally responsible for on-time payments
  • A missed payment affects both borrowers' credit profiles
  • Either borrower can be pursued for the full outstanding balance if the other defaults

What a Co-Borrower Cannot Do Unilaterally

Some actions require written consent from both parties, including removing a co-borrower from the loan, modifying loan terms, or initiating a loan transfer in connection with a home sale.
