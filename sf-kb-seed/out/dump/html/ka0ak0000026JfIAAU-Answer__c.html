Understanding a Denied Dispute

If your dispute is denied, you will receive an email with the subject line Dispute Decision: DSP-XXXXXXXX – Not Upheld. The email includes a plain-language explanation and the specific reason code assigned by our billing team. Common denial reason codes include:

CodeMeaning
DR-01Charge validated against your signed service agreement
DR-02Dispute submitted outside the 60-day window
DR-03Duplicate dispute already resolved
DR-04Insufficient supporting documentation

How to Appeal

  1. Reply directly to the denial email within 30 days and include any additional documentation (bank statements, prior correspondence, photos of equipment readings, etc.).
  2. Request escalation to a Senior Billing Specialist. Appeals are reviewed within 15 business days.
  3. If the appeal is also denied, you may request a final review by the Brightline Billing Oversight Team by calling 1-800-555-0192 and selecting option 4.

If You Cannot Pay While Awaiting Appeal

A denied dispute does not automatically trigger a late fee while an active appeal is on file. However, if the appeal is ultimately unsuccessful, standard late fees (1.5% per month) will be applied retroactively to the original due date. If paying the charge would create financial hardship, you may qualify for a payment deferral — see Brightline Hardship Program for eligibility details. For information on how refunds are processed once a dispute is upheld, see Refund Processing Time Update.
