Before you begin: You must be logged in to Brightline Home with the account used during your installation agreement. Installer contact details are populated automatically once your project is assigned an installation crew, typically within 3 business days of contract signing. If your project is still in the pre-assignment stage, the fields described below will display Pending Assignment.

  1. Open the Brightline Home app and tap the Menu icon (☰) in the top-left corner.
  2. Select Profile from the main navigation list.
  3. Scroll down to the My Installation Team section.
  4. You will see the following details: Team Name, Primary Phone (tap to dial directly), Support Email (tap to open your default mail app), and Office Hours (displayed in your local time zone).
  5. To save the phone number to your device contacts, tap the phone number and select Add to Contacts from the prompt.
  6. If you prefer to receive an email summary of this information, tap Send to My Email at the bottom of the section. The summary arrives within 5 minutes to your account's registered email address.

Troubleshooting

The My Installation Team section is missing entirely. This can occur if your account was created through a third-party referral portal and the project link has not yet synced. Log out, wait 10 minutes, and log back in. If the section still does not appear, contact Brightline Customer Care at 1-800-555-0192.

The phone number or email shown looks incorrect. Installer assignments can change when crews are reallocated across regions. Pull down on the Profile screen to force a refresh. If the data still appears wrong after 24 hours, tap Report Incorrect Info beneath the team card and submit the short form — our scheduling team will verify and correct the record within 1 business day.

If your installation appointment has already been affected, see how to check the status of a weather-delayed appointment in Brightline Home for additional status options.
