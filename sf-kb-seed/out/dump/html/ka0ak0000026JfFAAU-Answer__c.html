How Brightline breaks down a prorated invoice

When a prorated charge appears, your invoice splits the billing period into two or more partial-period segments. Each segment has its own line item so you can see exactly what you are being charged for and why.

Typical line items you will see

Line Item LabelWhat It Means
Monitoring Plan — ProratedThe daily rate for your plan multiplied by the number of days in that segment.
Daily RateYour monthly plan fee divided by the number of days in that calendar month. For example, a $24.90/month plan in a 30-day month has a daily rate of $0.83.
Segment DatesThe exact start and end dates covered by that line item, shown as MM/DD/YYYY–MM/DD/YYYY.
Previous Plan CreditA negative amount refunding unused days from a plan you downgraded or cancelled mid-cycle.
New Plan ChargeThe charge for the remaining days under your new plan or rate.

Where to find this detail in Brightline Home

  1. Open the app and tap Account → Billing → Invoice History.
  2. Select the invoice in question.
  3. Tap View Full Breakdown to expand all line items and segment dates.

If any amount still looks incorrect after reviewing the breakdown, contact Brightline Support at 1-800-555-0192 or start a chat in the app within 90 days of the invoice date to request a review.
