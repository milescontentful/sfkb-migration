Dispute Resolution Timeline

Once you submit a dispute through Brightline Home → Billing → Invoice History → [Invoice] → Dispute This Charge, our billing team begins review within 2 business days. The full process has three stages:

  1. Acknowledgment (within 2 business days): You'll receive an email and an in-app notification confirming your dispute reference number (format: DSP-XXXXXXXX).
  2. Active Review (up to 10 business days): A billing specialist examines your usage data, payment history, and any supporting documents you uploaded. If additional information is needed, they'll contact you via the email on your account — responding promptly keeps the clock from pausing.
  3. Decision (by business day 12): You'll be notified of the outcome in-app and by email. If the dispute is resolved in your favor, a credit or refund is applied within 5–7 business days.

While Your Dispute Is Open

The disputed amount is placed in a temporary hold status. Autopay will not process the held amount until the dispute closes, so you won't be charged twice. However, undisputed line items on the same invoice remain due on their original due date to avoid a late fee of $4.95.

Escalating a Delayed Dispute

If you haven't received a decision by business day 13, contact us at 1-800-BRIGHTLN or chat in the app with your DSP- reference number ready. For context on refund timing after a dispute is resolved in your favor, see Refund Processing Time Update. If you're concerned a dispute may be denied, review What Happens If My Invoice Dispute Is Denied.
