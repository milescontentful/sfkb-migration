What Happens When You Change Your Billing Date

Brightline Solar allows one billing-date change every 12 months at no fee. When you submit the change, our system closes your current billing cycle early and opens a new cycle that ends on your chosen date going forward. This creates a single transition invoice that covers the days between your old cycle-end and your new cycle-start.

Two Possible Transition Scenarios

ScenarioTransition PeriodResult
Moving billing date earlier (e.g., 15th → 1st)Less than 30 daysShort-cycle prorated charge for those days
Moving billing date later (e.g., 1st → 15th)More than 30 daysExtended-cycle invoice; net amount is still prorated per day, so you pay more once but less often initially

Example: Moving from the 15th to the 1st

Suppose your plan is $29.99/month and today is June 15. Your next normal invoice would have been July 15. Instead, we close the cycle on July 1—a 16-day period: ($29.99 ÷ 30) × 16 = $15.99. From August 1 onward, you receive standard full-month invoices on the 1st.

How to Request a Billing Date Change

  1. Open Brightline Home and navigate to Account → Billing → Billing Preferences.
  2. Tap Change Billing Date and select your preferred date (available options: 1st, 5th, 10th, 15th, 20th, or 25th).
  3. Review the transition invoice preview and tap Confirm.

The transition invoice is issued within 2 business days and is due 21 days after issuance. If you are enrolled in the Brightline Hardship Program and a billing-date change would create a hardship, contact support before confirming—see Hardship Program Expansion: Extended Deferral (January 2026) for deferral options. For general information on refund timing if a credit results from your transition, see Refund Processing Time Update (Payments 2025).
