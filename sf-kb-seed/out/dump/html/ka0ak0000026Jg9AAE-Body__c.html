What Is Changing

Firmware version RS-4.2.1 updates the rapid shutdown transmitter logic on all Brightline-installed systems manufactured after January 2022. The update rolls out in three waves:

  • Wave 1 (July 14–21, 2026): Systems in California, Arizona, and Nevada
  • Wave 2 (July 22–29, 2026): Systems in Texas, Florida, and Georgia
  • Wave 3 (July 30–August 6, 2026): All remaining states

New Alert Codes

After the update, rapid shutdown events will surface one of the following alert codes in Brightline Home → Alerts → System Safety:

CodeMeaningRecommended Action
RSD-01Voluntary shutdown triggered via appNo action required
RSD-02Grid-loss rapid shutdownWait for grid restoration
RSD-03Communication fault between transmitter and modulesContact support

What You Need to Do

No manual steps are required. Ensure your inverter remains powered and connected to Wi-Fi during your wave window. Updates typically complete within 12 minutes between midnight and 4 a.m. local time. If you receive an unexpected RSD-03 alert after the update, review our guidance on how to identify and dismiss a false alarm in Brightline Home before calling support.
