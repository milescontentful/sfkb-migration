Yes — A Designated Contact Can Provide Roof Access

You do not need to be personally present as long as a designated on-site contact is available to grant the technician access to your roof and any electrical panels that need to be reached. This person must be at least 18 years old and physically present for the full appointment window.

How to Register a Designated Contact

  1. Open Brightline Home and go to My Appointments.
  2. Tap the relevant appointment, then select Manage Access Contact.
  3. Enter the contact's full name and a direct phone number where they can be reached on appointment day.
  4. Tap Save Contact. You will receive a confirmation notification.

Changes must be submitted at least 24 hours before the scheduled appointment start time. Same-day changes cannot be processed through the app; call Brightline Scheduling Support at 1-833-274-5566 for urgent updates.

What Your Contact Should Know

  • They should be ready 15 minutes before the appointment window opens.
  • Technicians carry Brightline-branded ID badges — your contact may ask to see one.
  • They do not need to sign any documents unless a physical permit card must be posted; the app will notify you if this applies.

If no authorized contact is present when the technician arrives, the visit will be marked as a missed access event and a rescheduling fee of $75 may apply. For more on what to have ready before the crew arrives, see What do I need to do to prepare roof access for my Brightline appointment?
