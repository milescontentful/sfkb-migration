Preparing for Your Site Survey

A little preparation before your Brightline site survey helps our field technician complete the visit efficiently—usually within 60–90 minutes—and avoids the need for a follow-up trip that could delay your installation timeline.

What to Have Ready

  • Clear access to your main electrical panel. Remove any items stored in front of it. The technician needs at least 36 inches of clearance.
  • Attic or crawl space access. If your home has an attic hatch, make sure it is unobstructed. Our technician will inspect roof structure and insulation.
  • Recent utility bills. Have the last 12 months of electricity bills available, either printed or on your phone. This helps size your system accurately.
  • Roof access from outside. Unlock any side gates and ensure pets are secured so the technician can safely walk the perimeter.
  • A responsible adult on-site. Someone 18 or older must be present for the entire appointment. If your schedule changes, reschedule in advance to avoid a $75 missed-appointment fee.

Day-of Tips

  1. Confirm your appointment in the Brightline Home app under My Appointments > Upcoming at least 24 hours before.
  2. Watch for a technician arrival notification sent approximately 30 minutes before they reach your address.
