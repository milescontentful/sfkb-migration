What Changed

Previously, customers had to call Brightline Support (1-800-555-0193) and wait up to 5 business days to receive a payoff letter by email. Starting July 14, 2026, you can generate one in seconds directly inside the app.

How to Request Your Payoff Letter

  1. Open Brightline Home and tap Account → Financing → Loan Details.
  2. Tap Request Payoff Letter.
  3. Select your desired payoff date (up to 30 days in the future).
  4. Review the quoted payoff amount and tap Generate Letter.
  5. Download or share the signed PDF.
