Safety: do not open any enclosure. This procedure only uses the external switches and breakers.

Steps

  1. Locate the AC disconnect (grey box next to your utility meter) and switch it OFF.
  2. Locate the DC disconnect on the inverter and switch it OFF.
  3. Wait 5 minutes. The inverter display will go dark.
  4. Switch the DC disconnect ON.
  5. Switch the AC disconnect ON.
  6. Wait up to 10 minutes for the inverter to reconnect to the grid. The display should show a production reading.

If the alert persists after 30 minutes, submit a service request.
