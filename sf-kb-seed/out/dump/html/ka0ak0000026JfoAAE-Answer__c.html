Why Scheduled Reports Can Stop or Be Delayed

There are several common reasons a scheduled report may fail to arrive or arrive late. Work through the checks below to identify and resolve the issue.

1. The Report Was Accidentally Paused

Open Brightline Home > Reports > Scheduled and confirm the Active toggle is set to On for your report. A paused report will show a Paused badge in gray.

2. Email Address Changed or Recipient Inbox Is Full

If the destination address recently changed (for example, after an account email update) the scheduled report may be sending to your old address. Tap Edit Schedule on the affected report and verify all recipient addresses are correct.

3. Report Landed in Spam or Promotions

Check your spam, promotions, or junk folder for emails from reports@mail.brightlinesolar.com. Adding that address to your contacts prevents future filtering.

4. Temporary Delivery Delay

During periods of high platform load, scheduled reports may be queued and delivered up to 4 hours after their scheduled time. If your report arrived late but eventually appeared, no action is needed.

5. Data Sync Error (Error Code RPT-4402)

If your monitoring gateway lost connectivity during the reporting window, the system may skip that delivery and log error code RPT-4402. You will receive an in-app alert under Alerts > System. The next scheduled report will generate normally once connectivity is restored.

Still Not Receiving Reports?

Contact Brightline Support via Brightline Home > Help > Chat with Us and include your report name and the date of the last successful delivery. For information on lifetime stats you can review manually in the meantime, see How to View Your Lifetime Production Stats in Brightline Home. If you manage reports via the API, see How to Schedule Automated Report Delivery via the Partner API.
