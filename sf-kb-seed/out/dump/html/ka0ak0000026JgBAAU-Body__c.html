What Changed

On July 14, 2026, Brightline automatically updated default battery alert thresholds for all residential accounts with a Brightline-managed battery system. The changes apply to both push notifications and email alerts.

Alert TypeOld ThresholdNew Threshold
Low Charge Warning20% SOC12% SOC
Critical Low Charge10% SOC5% SOC
Charge Complete95% SOC98% SOC

How to Review or Restore Your Previous Settings

  • Open Brightline Home and navigate to Settings > Alerts > Battery.
  • Tap Edit Thresholds to set custom SOC values between 1% and 99%.
  • Tap Save to apply. Changes take effect within 5 minutes.

If you believe an alert fired incorrectly after this update, see our guide on how to identify and dismiss a false alarm in Brightline Home for troubleshooting steps.
