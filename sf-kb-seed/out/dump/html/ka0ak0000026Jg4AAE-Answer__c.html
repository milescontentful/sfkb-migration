Your Loan Agreement Does Not Change Automatically

A divorce decree or separation agreement is a legal document between you and your co-borrower, but it is not binding on Brightline. Until the loan is formally modified, both parties remain equally liable for all payments, regardless of what your divorce settlement specifies.

Options for Resolving the Loan

  • Co-borrower removal: Either party may submit a co-borrower removal request via Brightline Home > Account > Financing > Manage Co-Borrower. The remaining borrower must qualify for the full loan amount through a new credit review. Processing takes approximately 15–20 business days. A $75 loan modification fee applies unless waived by a Brightline financing specialist.
  • Full payoff: One party may pay off the loan in full. Request a payoff letter at least 5 business days before the intended payoff date.
  • Home sale and loan transfer: If the property is being sold, the loan may be paid off from sale proceeds or, in eligible cases, transferred to a qualified buyer.

What to Expect During Review

During the co-borrower removal review period, the account remains active and payments are still due on their normal schedule. Late payments during this period will affect both parties' credit.
