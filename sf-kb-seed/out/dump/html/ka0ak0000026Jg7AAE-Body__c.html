What's Changing

Brightline Home is updating its supported browser matrix on March 1, 2026. The following browsers are affected:

Why We're Making This Change

Legacy browsers lack support for the WebAuthn and TLS 1.3 standards that power Brightline Home's secure login flow. Removing them lets us enforce stronger encryption and faster page loads for all users.

What You Should Do

  • Check your current browser version in its About menu.
  • Update to the latest stable release before March 1, 2026.
  • If you experience login errors after updating, see Account Lockout Policy Update (Login 2026) for related troubleshooting steps.
