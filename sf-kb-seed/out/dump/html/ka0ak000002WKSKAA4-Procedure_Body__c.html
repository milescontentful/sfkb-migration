Before you start: have your bank routing and account number, or a card, ready. Autopay drafts on the 10th of each month.

Steps

  1. Open Brightline Home and go to Billing → Payment methods.
  2. Tap Add payment method and choose Bank account or Card.
  3. Enter the details and tap Verify. Bank accounts receive two micro-deposits within 2 business days; return to this screen to confirm the amounts.
  4. Once verified, toggle Use for autopay.
  5. Confirm the authorization text and tap Enable autopay.

To turn autopay off

Return to Payment methods, open the method, and toggle Use for autopay off. Do this at least 3 business days before the 10th to avoid that month's draft.
