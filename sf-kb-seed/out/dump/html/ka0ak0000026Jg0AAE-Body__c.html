New Webhook Events

Published July 1, 2026, the following events are now emitted under the ownership_transfer event group:

  • site.ownership_transfer.created — fired immediately on successful POST
  • site.ownership_transfer.docs_received — fired when all required documents are uploaded
  • site.ownership_transfer.status_changed — fired on any status transition
  • site.ownership_transfer.completed — fired when new owner access is activated
  • site.ownership_transfer.cancelled — fired on DELETE or system cancellation

Retry Guidance

Webhook delivery for ownership transfer events follows the standard retry policy. Review the March 2026 webhook retry policy update for backoff intervals and failure thresholds. Partners should treat OT_TRANSFER_IN_PROGRESS (409) responses as non-retryable until the existing transfer resolves.
