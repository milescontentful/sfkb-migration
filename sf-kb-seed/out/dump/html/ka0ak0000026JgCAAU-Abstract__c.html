Brightline Solar has deployed an update to its alert engine that introduces a 4-minute confirmation window before a battery disconnection alert is sent. Previously, any communication gap longer than 90 seconds triggered a BATT_OFFLINE_001 alert, causing a high volume of false alarms for customers whose home network or cellular gateway experienced brief interruptions. As of August 5, 2026, the system waits 4 minutes of sustained silence before issuing the alert, and automatically clears it without a separate notification once connectivity is restored.
