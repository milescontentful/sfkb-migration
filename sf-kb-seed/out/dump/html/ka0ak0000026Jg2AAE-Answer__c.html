Making a Co-Borrower Change

Co-borrower modifications on an active Brightline Solar loan are treated as a loan assumption or restructure and require underwriting review. You cannot complete this change entirely on your own — a Brightline Financing Specialist must open a case on your behalf.

Step-by-Step: Adding a Co-Borrower

  1. Open Brightline Home and go to Account → Financing → Manage Co-Borrower → Add Co-Borrower.
  2. Enter the prospective co-borrower's name and email. They will receive a secure invitation to submit their information directly.
  3. Upload required documents (see related article below for eligibility details).
  4. A Brightline Financing Specialist will contact both parties within 3 business days to confirm next steps.
  5. Once underwriting approves, both parties sign an amended loan agreement via DocuSign. The amendment is recorded within 10–15 business days.

Step-by-Step: Removing a Co-Borrower

  1. Navigate to Account → Financing → Manage Co-Borrower → Request Removal.
  2. The remaining borrower must qualify for the full loan balance independently — a new credit check (hard pull) will be initiated.
  3. If approved, a loan modification document is issued for e-signature. The removed co-borrower receives written confirmation within 5 business days of recording.

Fees

  • Co-borrower addition: $75 loan modification fee
  • Co-borrower removal: $125 loan restructure fee
  • Fees are added to your next monthly invoice and are non-refundable once underwriting begins.

Note: Co-borrower changes do not affect your interest rate or remaining loan term unless a full refinance is requested.
