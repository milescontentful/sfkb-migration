Before you begin: You will need the Brightline Home app (version 4.2.0 or later) installed on your mobile device and an active internet connection. Only the account owner or an authorized household member with Alert permissions can dismiss alerts.

  1. Open the Brightline Home app and tap Alerts in the bottom navigation bar. Active alerts appear at the top under Needs Attention.
  2. Tap the alert you want to investigate. Review the alert detail screen, which shows the alert code (for example, ALT-204: Low Production Warning), the time it triggered, and the affected component.
  3. Tap Check System Status on the alert detail screen. The app polls your inverter and gateway in real time. If all readings return green within 60 seconds, the alert is likely a false alarm caused by a brief communication dropout or a transient grid fluctuation.
  4. Scroll down to the Possible Causes section. Look for entries labeled Transient — no action required. These are pre-classified false-alarm patterns based on your system's telemetry history.
  5. Tap Mark as False Alarm, then select a reason from the dropdown (options include Temporary shading, Grid momentary outage, App connectivity gap, or Other). Adding a brief note is optional but helps Brightline improve alert accuracy.
  6. Tap Confirm Dismiss. The alert moves to Alert History with a False Alarm label and is excluded from your monthly Alert Summary report.

Troubleshooting

The Mark as False Alarm button is grayed out. This occurs when an alert carries a Critical severity flag (code prefix ALT-5xx). Critical alerts cannot be self-dismissed; contact Brightline Support at 1-800-555-0192 so a technician can verify the issue before it is cleared.

The system status check times out. If the real-time poll does not complete within 90 seconds, your gateway may be offline. Check that your home Wi-Fi is active and that the Brightline gateway LED is solid blue. A flashing amber LED indicates the gateway is reconnecting; wait two minutes and retry. If the problem persists, see How to Track and Respond to Your Inspection Day Results in Brightline Home to log a follow-up service request.

You dismissed an alert by mistake. Go to Alerts > Alert History, find the entry, and tap Reopen Alert within 24 hours of dismissal to restore it to Needs Attention status.
