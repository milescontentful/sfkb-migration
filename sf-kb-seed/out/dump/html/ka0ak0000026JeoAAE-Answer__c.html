When a Revised Estimate Is Issued Automatically

Brightline automatically generates a new production estimate whenever a permitted system modification is completed and passes final inspection. This includes panel expansions, full inverter replacements, and battery additions that alter your system's rated capacity. The revised estimate typically appears in Brightline Home within 3–5 business days after your utility interconnection approval is updated in our records.

You will receive an in-app notification (under Reports > Production Estimates) and an email confirming the new estimate is ready.

When You Need to Request a Revision

Some changes are not captured automatically and require a manual request:

  • Significant new shading (e.g., a neighbor's new addition or mature tree growth)
  • Panel cleaning or derating corrections after a soiling audit
  • Corrections to roof orientation data identified after installation

To submit a revision request, go to Brightline Home > Reports > Production Estimates > Request Revision and select the reason from the dropdown. A Brightline energy analyst will review your case within 7 business days and issue a revised report or contact you for a site re-assessment.
