1. Go to Account → Security.
  2. Tap Two-factor authentication → Set up.
  3. Choose Authenticator app (recommended) or SMS.
  4. Scan the QR code or enter the phone number, then enter the 6-digit code.
  5. Save your backup codes. You'll need one if you lose your phone.

Warning: enabling 2FA signs you out of all devices, including household members' devices.
