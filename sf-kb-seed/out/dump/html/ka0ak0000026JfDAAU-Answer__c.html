What Is a Prorated Charge?

A prorated charge covers a partial billing period rather than a full calendar month. Brightline Solar calculates your monthly monitoring, maintenance plan, or battery management fee on a 30-day cycle. When an event causes your billing period to begin or end on a day other than your normal billing date, we charge only for the days of service you actually received.

Common Reasons You May See a Prorated Charge

  • System activation mid-month: Your system went live on a day other than your regular billing date, so your first invoice covers only the days from activation through the end of that cycle.
  • Plan upgrade or downgrade: Switching between monitoring tiers (e.g., from Brightline Essential to Brightline Plus) triggers a prorated credit for unused days on the old plan and a prorated charge for days on the new plan.
  • Home sale or transfer: Service ending before a billing period closes results in a final prorated invoice for remaining days.

How the Amount Is Calculated

The formula is straightforward:

Prorated Amount = (Monthly Plan Rate ÷ 30) × Number of Active Days

For example, if your Brightline Plus plan is $24.99/month and you activated on the 11th of a 30-day cycle, you are billed for 20 days: ($24.99 ÷ 30) × 20 = $16.66.

Where to Find the Breakdown

Open the Brightline Home app, tap Account → Billing → Invoice History, and select the invoice in question. Each prorated line item shows the start date, end date, daily rate, and day count.

If you believe the dates shown are incorrect, contact Brightline Support at 1-800-555-0192 within 30 days of the invoice date. For context on how charges are handled during a home sale, see Who is responsible for the solar system during the home sale closing period?
