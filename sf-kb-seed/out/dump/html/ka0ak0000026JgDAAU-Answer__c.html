What Is a Site Survey?

A site survey is a mandatory pre-installation assessment conducted by a certified Brightline field technician before your solar system design is finalized. It typically takes 60–90 minutes, though larger homes or complex roof layouts may require up to 2 hours.

What the Technician Will Inspect

  • Roof structure: Material type (shingle, tile, metal), pitch, condition, and available surface area for panel placement.
  • Electrical panel: Current amperage rating, available breaker slots, and compatibility with your planned system size.
  • Attic access: Rafter spacing and structural integrity for mounting hardware.
  • Shading analysis: Using a calibrated solar pathfinder tool, the technician maps obstructions such as trees or neighboring structures across all daylight hours.
  • Utility meter and interconnection point: Location and accessibility for the inverter and production meter installation.

What You Should Prepare

  1. Ensure an adult (18+) is present for the full duration of the visit.
  2. Clear a 3-foot perimeter around your main electrical panel.
  3. Provide attic access if a pull-down staircase or hatch is the only entry point.
  4. Have a recent utility bill available — the technician may ask about your average monthly usage.

After the Survey

Results are uploaded to your Brightline account within 2 business days. You will receive an in-app notification in Brightline Home under My Project > Survey Results once your customized system design is ready for review.

If the technician identifies a concern (such as a roof needing repair before installation), you will receive a separate Pre-Installation Requirements notice via email and in-app alert.
