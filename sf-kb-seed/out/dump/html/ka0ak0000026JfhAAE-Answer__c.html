Supported Languages

Brightline Home currently supports the following languages:

  • English (US) – default
  • Spanish (Español)
  • French (Français)
  • Portuguese (Português – Brasil)
  • Vietnamese (Tiếng Việt)
  • Simplified Chinese (中文 – 简体)

Additional languages are evaluated each quarter based on customer demand. If your preferred language is not listed, you can submit a request through Help & Feedback → Feature Request inside the app.

Where to Change Your Language

To switch languages, open the app and navigate to Profile → App Preferences → Display Language. Select your preferred language from the list and tap Save. The app will reload automatically and apply the new language within a few seconds. No internet reconnection is required.

What Changes When You Switch Languages

Changing your display language affects:

  • All menus, buttons, and navigation labels
  • In-app notifications and alert messages
  • On-screen help text and tooltips

The following items are not affected by your language setting and will continue to appear in English:

  • Legal documents and terms of service
  • Invoice line-item descriptions
  • Partner API responses

For details on resetting after an unexpected language change, see Why did my Brightline Home language reset to English?
