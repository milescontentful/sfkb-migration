Ground-Mount Permits Are Different from Rooftop Permits

Yes — ground-mount systems require a separate and generally more complex permit package than rooftop installations. Because a ground-mount involves excavation, concrete footings, and a freestanding structure, most jurisdictions treat it as both an electrical permit and a structural/building permit, and some counties also require a grading or land-disturbance permit if more than 50 square feet of soil is disturbed.

What Brightline Submits on Your Behalf

Your Brightline project coordinator handles all permit applications. The standard ground-mount permit package includes:

  • Site plan showing setbacks, easements, and panel layout
  • Structural engineering stamp for the racking system (PE-stamped drawings, required in 38 of the 50 states we serve)
  • Electrical single-line diagram
  • Soil report summary if the jurisdiction requires it
  • Zoning compliance letter, where applicable

Typical Timeline

Ground-mount permit approval averages 4–8 weeks, compared to 1–3 weeks for rooftop permits. Jurisdictions with an HOA overlay or agricultural zoning can extend this to 10–12 weeks. Your Brightline Home app will show real-time permit status under My Project → Permits & Approvals.

Fees

Most permit fees are included in your Brightline installation contract. If a jurisdiction charges an additional structural review fee above $350, Brightline will notify you before submission and itemize the charge.
