Finding Your Updated Appointment

  1. Open Brightline Home and tap My Projects in the bottom navigation bar.
  2. Select the relevant project card (e.g., System Installation or Service Visit).
  3. Tap Appointments. Weather-delayed visits display a yellow Weather Hold badge alongside the new proposed date and a countdown timer.
  4. Tap the appointment row to see the full detail view, including the weather condition that triggered the delay and the crew's estimated arrival window.

Enabling Weather-Delay Alerts

To ensure you receive immediate notifications for any future changes:

  1. Go to Settings > Notifications > Scheduling Alerts.
  2. Toggle Weather Delay Updates to On.
  3. Choose your preferred channel: Push, Email, or Both.

If Your New Date Does Not Work

Tap Reschedule on the appointment detail screen to choose an available slot. Reschedules caused by weather carry no fee, and your place in the installation queue is preserved. If you need to grant a crew member access to your roof for the rescheduled visit, review How to Grant Roof Access for a Scheduled Service Visit. You can also revisit original scheduling steps at How to Reschedule or Cancel Your Site Survey.

Still Not Seeing an Updated Date?

If your appointment still shows Weather Hold with no new date after 48 hours, contact Brightline Support via the in-app chat or call 1-800-555-0174.
