Enabling Automatic Payment Receipts

Yes — Brightline Home can send you an email receipt automatically every time a payment is successfully processed, whether it's a scheduled autopay, a one-time manual payment, or a retry after a previously failed charge.

How to turn on automatic receipts

  1. Open Brightline Home and tap Account in the bottom navigation bar.
  2. Select Billing & Payments, then tap Notification Preferences.
  3. Under the Receipt Emails section, toggle Send receipt after every successful payment to On.
  4. Confirm the email address shown is correct. To change it, tap Edit Email and save.

What the receipt includes

  • Payment confirmation number (format: BL-RCT-XXXXXXXX)
  • Payment date, amount, and method (e.g., ACH, credit card ending in ····4821)
  • Invoice number(s) the payment was applied to
  • Your account balance after the payment

Receipt delivery timing

Receipts are generated and emailed within 5 minutes of a successful payment. If a payment is settled overnight (common for ACH), the receipt arrives by 6:00 AM local time the following business day.

If you're not receiving receipts even with the toggle enabled, check your spam folder for emails from receipts@mail.brightlinesolar.com, then verify your notification settings are saved. For details on what to do if a receipt shows an unexpected amount, see Why does my payment receipt show a different amount than I expected?
