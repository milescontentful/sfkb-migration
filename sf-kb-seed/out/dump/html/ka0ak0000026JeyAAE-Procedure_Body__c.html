Before you begin: Your inspection must already have taken place. Results are posted to Brightline Home within 2 business days of the inspection date. You will receive a push notification and an email to your account address when the result is available. Make sure notifications are enabled under Settings > Notifications > Permit Updates in the app.

  1. Open Brightline Home and tap My Project in the bottom navigation bar.
  2. Tap Permits, then tap Inspection History. Your most recent inspection appears at the top with a status badge: Passed (green), Failed – Brightline Action Required (amber), or Failed – Homeowner Action Required (red).
  3. Tap the inspection record to open the detail screen. You can view the inspector's notes, the permit number, and the inspection reference code (format: INS-XXXXXX).
  4. To download your official Certificate of Inspection, tap Download PDF. Store this document; your utility company will request it during interconnection review.
  5. If status is Passed: No action is needed on your part. Brightline automatically submits your interconnection package to your utility within 3 business days. You will receive a separate notification when interconnection is submitted. To learn more about what happens next, see What Is Utility Interconnection and Why Does It Take So Long.
  6. If status is Failed – Brightline Action Required: A Brightline technician will contact you within 1 business day to schedule a remediation and re-inspection visit. No fee applies. Monitor My Project > Permits for the new inspection date once booked.
  7. If status is Failed – Homeowner Action Required: The detail screen will list the specific correction needed (for example, replacing a non-code-compliant smoke detector or clearing a required clearance zone). Complete the listed items and then tap Request Re-Inspection to trigger scheduling. A $95 re-inspection coordination fee will be applied to your next billing cycle.
  8. After a successful re-inspection, repeat steps 1–5 to confirm the updated status and download the revised certificate.

Troubleshooting

Result not showing after 2 business days. First confirm the inspection took place by checking your confirmation email for the INS-XXXXXX reference code. If you have the code but see no result, tap My Project > Permits > Refresh or force-close and reopen the app. If the issue persists, contact Brightline Support with your reference code.

Download PDF button is greyed out. The certificate is generated by the municipality and may take an additional 1–2 business days beyond the result posting. If it remains unavailable after 4 business days, contact Support to request a manual document pull.

Utility rejects the package after a passed inspection. This is a separate process from the municipal inspection. See What Happens If My Utility Rejects My Interconnection Application for next steps.
