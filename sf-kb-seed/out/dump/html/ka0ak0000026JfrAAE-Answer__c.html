Typical Rescheduling Timeframes

When Brightline Solar postpones an installation due to unsafe weather conditions—such as high winds (sustained speeds above 25 mph), active precipitation, or lightning within 10 miles of the job site—our scheduling team begins rebooking affected appointments within 1 business day of the original date. In most cases, your new appointment will fall within 5–14 calendar days of the cancelled visit, depending on crew availability and local permit validity.

Factors That Can Extend the Wait

  • Regional weather patterns: Prolonged storm systems (e.g., multi-day rain events) may push your slot further out if multiple crews are affected simultaneously.
  • Permit expiration windows: If your installation permit expires within 30 days of the delay, a Brightline permit coordinator will contact you separately to discuss extension options before rescheduling.
  • Roof-access requirements: Delays may be longer if your property requires a scheduled access window. Review how to grant roof access for a scheduled service visit to ensure your access credentials remain active.
  • Equipment staging: Pre-staged equipment at a local warehouse is held for up to 21 days before it must be reassigned to another job.

What to Expect Next

You will receive an automated push notification and email from the Brightline Home app with your new proposed appointment time. You can accept, request a different date, or view delay status details directly in the app. For step-by-step status tracking, see how to check the status of a weather-delayed appointment in Brightline Home.
