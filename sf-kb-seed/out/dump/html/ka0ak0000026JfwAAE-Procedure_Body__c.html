Before you begin: Only the primary Account Owner can update installer contact preferences. If you are an Authorized User on the account, you will see this section in read-only mode. Have your new phone number or email address ready. Changes take effect within 2 hours and will be visible to your installation crew on their scheduling dashboard by the next business day.

  1. Open Brightline Home and tap the Menu icon (☰), then select Profile.
  2. Tap Communication Preferences near the top of the Profile screen.
  3. Under the Installer Contact heading, tap Edit.
  4. To change your phone number, tap the Phone Number field, clear the existing entry, and type your new 10-digit US number. Brightline Home will send a 6-digit SMS verification code to that number; enter the code in the Verify Number field that appears and tap Confirm.
  5. To change your email address, tap the Email Address field and enter the new address. A verification link will be sent to the new address; click that link within 24 hours to confirm. Until confirmed, your original email remains active for installer communications.
  6. Select your Preferred Contact Method using the toggle: Phone, Email, or Either. Your installation crew will honor this preference for non-urgent outreach.
  7. Tap Save Changes. A green confirmation banner reading Installer contact preferences updated will appear at the top of the screen.

Troubleshooting

You did not receive the SMS verification code. Wait 2 minutes, then tap Resend Code. Codes expire after 10 minutes. If you still do not receive a code, confirm the number you entered is a US mobile number capable of receiving SMS — VoIP numbers are not supported for verification.

The Save Changes button is grayed out. This usually means a verification step is still pending. Check that both the phone and email fields show a green check mark before saving. If one field shows a yellow warning icon, tap it to see the specific error code (common codes: CONTACT-401 — duplicate number already on file; CONTACT-403 — restricted number format).

Changes are not reflected after 2 hours. Force-refresh the Profile screen by pulling down. If the old contact details persist, log out completely and log back in. Persistent issues should be reported to Brightline Customer Care.
