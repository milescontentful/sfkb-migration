Announcement Details

Effective Date: April 15, 2026

This release resolves error code BH-LOGIN-4031 ("OAuth redirect blocked by in-app browser") that affected users who clicked login links directly from their email apps on iOS and Android.

Newly Supported Mobile Browsers

  • Chrome 120 or later on Android 10+
  • Safari 16 or later on iOS 15+
  • Samsung Internet 22 or later
  • Firefox for Android 121 or later

Known Limitations

  • Biometric login (Face ID / fingerprint) is supported only in native Safari and Chrome, not in third-party in-app browsers.
  • Battery Control scheduling requires a viewport width of at least 360px; some older budget Android devices may see a degraded layout.

What to Do If Login Still Fails

If you receive error code BH-LOGIN-4031 after April 15, open the Brightline Home link in your device's default browser rather than your email app. For MFA-related login issues, review Temporary Account Lockout & MFA Enforcement (Login 2026). Contact support at 1-800-555-0192 for further help.
