Sections of Your Brightline Loan Statement

Your monthly loan statement is generated on the first business day after your billing cycle closes and covers the prior calendar month. It is available in the Brightline Home app under Account → Financing → Loan Statements and is also emailed as a PDF to your address on file.

Statement Header

The header contains your Loan Account Number (format: BL-LN-XXXXXXXX), statement date, payment due date, and the name of the lending program (e.g., Brightline SunFlex 25-Year).

Account Summary Table

FieldDescription
Previous BalanceOutstanding principal + accrued interest at the start of the period
Payments ReceivedPayments posted during the statement period
Interest ChargedInterest accrued at your fixed APR for the period
Current BalanceTotal amount owed as of the statement date
Minimum Payment DueAmount required to avoid a late fee ($18.00 after a 10-day grace period)

Payment Activity Log

Every payment, credit, or adjustment posted during the period is listed with a date, reference number, and method (ACH, check, or card).

Interest Rate Disclosure

Your current fixed APR, the daily periodic rate, and the number of days in the billing cycle are disclosed in this section as required by federal lending regulations.
