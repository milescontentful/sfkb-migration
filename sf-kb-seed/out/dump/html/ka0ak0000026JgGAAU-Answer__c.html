Site Survey Scheduling Window

Once your Brightline Solar contract is countersigned and your account is activated, you can book a site survey as early as the next business day and as far out as 90 calendar days from the contract date. Appointments are available Monday through Saturday, 8 AM – 5 PM in your local time zone.

How to Book

  1. Open the Brightline Home app and tap My Project in the bottom navigation bar.
  2. Select Schedule Site Survey. If this option is grayed out, your contract countersignature is still processing—check back within one business day.
  3. Choose a date and two-hour arrival window, then confirm. You will receive an email and push notification with your confirmation number (format: SS-XXXXXXX).

Factors That Affect Availability

  • Regional demand: Slots in high-volume markets like California and Texas may fill 2–3 weeks out, especially April through August.
  • Technician coverage area: Rural addresses may have limited weekday availability; Saturday slots are often more open.
  • Weather holds: During active weather advisories, same-week slots may be automatically blocked. See Why was my installation appointment delayed due to weather? for more information.

Need to Change Your Appointment?

You can reschedule at no charge up to 48 hours before your appointment start time. For rescheduling and cancellation details, visit How do I reschedule or cancel my site survey appointment?
