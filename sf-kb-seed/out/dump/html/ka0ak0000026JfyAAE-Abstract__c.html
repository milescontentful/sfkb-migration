Beginning June 2, 2026, eligible Brightline Solar customers can choose between an in-person or virtual post-install walkthrough when scheduling through the Brightline Home app. The virtual format uses a 45-minute live video session with a certified Brightline technician who guides customers through the same checklist as an in-person visit, including production monitoring setup, alert configuration, and battery controls. Eligibility is determined automatically based on system type, inverter model, and local jurisdiction requirements; customers who do not qualify will only see the in-person option when booking.
