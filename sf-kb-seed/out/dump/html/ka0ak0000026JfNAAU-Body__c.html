Effective March 1, 2026, Brightline Solar's payment processing center in Phoenix, AZ is updating its check handling procedures to comply with new banking partner requirements. As a result, mailed check payments will take up to 14 business days to post to your Brightline account — up from the previous 7-business-day window.

Key Dates and Guidance

  • Mail your check at least 16 business days before your due date to ensure on-time posting.
  • Checks must be made payable to Brightline Solar LLC.
  • Include your 10-digit account number (found in Brightline Home under Account > Billing > Account Details) in the memo line.
  • Send checks to: Brightline Solar Payments, PO Box 48210, Phoenix, AZ 85072.
