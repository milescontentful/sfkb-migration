When a prorated charge can be reversed

Brightline will issue a correction if the prorated charge resulted from any of the following:

  • A plan change or billing-date update that was processed on the wrong date due to a system error.
  • A duplicate charge for the same billing segment.
  • An incorrect daily rate caused by a pricing configuration error on Brightline's side.
  • A service interruption of 48 hours or more that was not excluded from billing under your service agreement.

How to open a dispute

  1. In Brightline Home, go to Account → Billing → Invoice History and open the affected invoice.
  2. Tap Dispute This Charge and select the specific line item you believe is incorrect.
  3. Provide a brief description and, if available, attach a screenshot or note the date you made the plan change.
  4. Submit the form. You will receive a confirmation email with a case number (format: BL-INV-XXXXXXX) within 15 minutes.

What happens next

Brightline's billing team reviews disputes within 5–7 business days. If the charge is confirmed incorrect, you will receive either:

  • A bill credit applied to your next invoice (most common), or
  • A direct refund to your original payment method within 7–10 business days, if your account balance is zero.

Disputes must be submitted within 90 days of the invoice date; charges older than 90 days cannot be reviewed except in cases of documented system error.
