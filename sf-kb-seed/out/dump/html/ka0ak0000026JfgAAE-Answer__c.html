Why This Happens

In rare cases, a major app version update (version increments such as 3.x → 4.x) can reset certain profile preferences, including language, to their defaults. This is a known issue tracked under internal reference BLH-PREF-412 and is most likely to occur when the update includes a full rebuild of the preferences data schema.

How to Restore Your Language Setting

  1. Open Brightline Home and tap your profile avatar in the top-right corner.
  2. Go to Account Settings → Preferences → Language & Region.
  3. Select your preferred language and tap Save.

Preventing Future Resets

To protect your preference across future updates, enable Cloud Preference Sync:

  • Navigate to Account Settings → Preferences → Sync & Backup.
  • Toggle Cloud Preference Sync to On.

With this setting enabled, your language choice is stored server-side and automatically reapplied after any app update within 60 seconds of first launch.

Still Experiencing Resets?

If the language reverts more than once after enabling Cloud Preference Sync, contact Brightline Support at 1-833-274-7652 or use Help & Support → Chat in the app. Have your account email and the app version number ready (found under Settings → About Brightline Home).
