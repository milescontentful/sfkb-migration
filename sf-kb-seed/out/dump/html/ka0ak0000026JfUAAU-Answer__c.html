Payment Application Order

When a partial payment is received, Brightline applies funds in the following fixed priority order:

  1. Outstanding late fees – any assessed 1.5% monthly late fees are cleared first.
  2. Oldest past-due invoice balance – the invoice with the earliest original due date is paid down next.
  3. Subsequent past-due invoices – additional aged balances are paid in chronological order.
  4. Current (not yet due) invoice balance – any remaining funds are applied last.

This order is automatic and cannot be manually overridden at this time. If you need a specific allocation, you must pay each invoice individually using the custom amount field on the per-invoice payment screen (Billing → [Invoice] → Make a Payment → Enter a custom amount).

Viewing Payment Allocation

To see exactly how your payment was distributed:

  • Go to Billing → Payment History.
  • Tap the payment entry to expand the Allocation Breakdown panel, which lists each invoice or fee credited and the exact dollar amount applied.

This breakdown is also included in the emailed receipt sent within 15 minutes of payment processing.

Disputing an Unexpected Allocation

If a payment was applied in a way that seems incorrect—for example, to a fee you believe was already waived—you can open a billing dispute in-app under Billing → Help → Dispute a Charge. Resolutions are typically issued within 5 business days.

See also: How to Dispute a Charge and How to Get a Payment Receipt.
