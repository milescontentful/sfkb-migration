Effective date: April 14, 2026

The post-install walkthrough is a required step before your Brightline Home monitoring dashboard activates live production data. During the appointment, your technician will:

  • Confirm inverter communication and verify real-time production readings in the app
  • Walk you through battery charge/discharge settings (if applicable)
  • Review alert thresholds and show you how to dismiss or adjust notifications
  • Confirm billing cycle start date and answer billing questions

Scheduling Window

MilestoneDeadline
PTO issuedDay 0
Must schedule walkthroughWithin 7 days of PTO
Walkthrough must be completedWithin 21 days of PTO

To book, open Brightline Home, tap My System > Appointments > Schedule Walkthrough, and select an available two-hour window. Rescheduling is free up to 24 hours before the appointment; cancellations inside 24 hours may incur a $35 late-cancel fee. For questions about what your installer will cover during the visit, see How to view your installer contact information in Brightline Home.
