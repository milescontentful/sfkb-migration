Why Documentation Matters

Providing clear evidence upfront allows our billing specialists to resolve disputes faster — often within 5 business days instead of the standard 12. Disputes submitted without supporting documents are still accepted but typically take longer.

Recommended Documents by Dispute Type

Dispute ReasonSuggested Documents
Charge amount doesn't match my planYour signed service agreement, original quote PDF
Payment already madeBank or credit card statement showing the debit, payment confirmation email
Prorated charge seems incorrectScreenshot of your billing date change confirmation in-app
Equipment-related billing errorPhotos of system status screen, microinverter offline alerts
Credit or refund not appliedPrior dispute resolution email, credit confirmation notice

How to Upload Documents

  1. In Brightline Home, go to Billing → Invoice History.
  2. Select the invoice and tap Dispute This Charge.
  3. On the dispute form, tap Add Supporting Files. Accepted formats: PDF, JPG, PNG (max 10 MB each, up to 5 files).
  4. Add a brief description in the Notes field explaining what each file shows.
  5. Tap Submit Dispute to finalize.

After You Submit

You'll receive a dispute reference number (DSP-XXXXXXXX) by email. If your dispute involves a prorated charge, also review Can I Get a Refund or Credit for a Prorated Charge I Was Billed Incorrectly. For refund eligibility rules that apply once a dispute is resolved, see Refund Eligibility Policy Clarification.
