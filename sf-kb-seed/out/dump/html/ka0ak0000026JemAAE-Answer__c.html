Overview of Report Time Periods

Your production estimate report in Brightline Home (Reports → Production Estimates) is organized into three nested time horizons, each serving a different planning purpose.

Monthly Estimates

The Monthly tab shows expected kWh output for each calendar month based on your location's average solar irradiance profile. Summer months will show higher estimates than winter months. These figures update automatically if Brightline recalibrates your system model — you will see a Revised badge on any month that has been updated since your original installation estimate.

Annual Estimates

The Annual tab aggregates monthly figures into a full-year total and compares it against your actual metered production year-to-date. A green indicator means you are tracking at or above estimate; amber means 5–14% below; red means 15%+ below and may trigger a proactive support outreach from Brightline.

25-Year Projection

The 25-Year Projection tab models cumulative production across your system's expected lifespan, applying a 0.5% annual degradation rate to each successive year. This view also shows estimated lifetime savings in kWh and, where applicable, estimated utility bill offset in dollars based on the rate you provided during onboarding.

Accuracy and Revisions

  • Monthly and annual estimates carry a stated accuracy range of ±8%.
  • 25-year projections carry a wider range of ±15% due to long-term irradiance and rate uncertainty.
  • Estimates can be revised after a system expansion, panel replacement, or tariff change — you will receive an in-app notification when a revision occurs.
