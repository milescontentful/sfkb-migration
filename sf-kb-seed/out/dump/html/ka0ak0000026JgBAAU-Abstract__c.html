Effective July 14, 2026, Brightline Solar updated the default alert thresholds for battery state-of-charge (SOC) warnings in the Brightline Home app. The previous default low-charge alert triggered at 20% SOC, which generated frequent alerts during normal evening discharge cycles. The new default threshold is 12% SOC, with a critical alert firing at 5% SOC. Customers can still customize these values under Settings > Alerts > Battery in the app. No action is required unless you prefer to keep your old thresholds.
