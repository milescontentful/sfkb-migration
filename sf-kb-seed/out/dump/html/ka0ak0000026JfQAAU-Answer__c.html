Common Reasons for an Unexpected Receipt Amount

Several factors can cause the amount on your receipt to differ from your expected payment. The most frequent reasons are listed below.

ReasonWhat to Look For
Prorated charge appliedLine item labeled Prorated Adjustment on your invoice
Outstanding balance rolled overLine item labeled Previous Balance
Credit or discount appliedNegative line item labeled Account Credit
Returned payment fee ($25.00)Line item labeled Returned Payment Fee

Prorated Charges

If you recently changed your billing date or your service agreement start date falls mid-cycle, a prorated amount is added or subtracted automatically. See what line items appear on my invoice when I have a prorated charge for a detailed breakdown of how these appear on your receipt.

Credits Reducing Your Total

A loyalty credit, referral reward, or hardship program adjustment may reduce your receipt total below your standard monthly amount. Credits are displayed as negative values and are applied before the payment is processed.

If You Believe the Amount Is Incorrect

If none of the above explanations account for the discrepancy, you may have grounds to dispute the charge. Review how to dispute a charge on your Brightline invoice for step-by-step instructions. Contact Brightline Support with your receipt number (format: RCP-XXXXXXXX) and a brief description of the discrepancy.
