No Fee for Weather-Initiated Cancellations

If Brightline Solar cancels or postpones your appointment due to weather conditions, you will not be charged any cancellation or rescheduling fee. Weather holds are logged in our system with reason code WX-HOLD, which automatically suppresses any fee triggers associated with that appointment slot.

How This Differs From Customer-Initiated Changes

Brightline's standard policy charges a $75 late-reschedule fee when a customer cancels or moves an appointment with fewer than 48 hours' notice. Weather cancellations initiated by Brightline are entirely separate and are never counted against your reschedule history. Your account will show the cancellation as Brightline-initiated in the Appointments section of the Brightline Home app under Menu > My Appointments > History.

What If I Want to Reschedule to a Different Date After a Weather Hold?

Once Brightline sends your proposed new appointment, you have 72 hours to accept or request an alternate date at no charge. If you request a second date change beyond that window, standard rescheduling terms apply. To understand all reschedule and cancellation options, see how to reschedule or cancel your site survey.
