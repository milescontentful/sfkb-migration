Before you begin: You must have an active Brightline account with a signed solar agreement and have completed your welcome call with your project coordinator. Site surveys are typically available within 5–10 business days of your agreement activation date. You will need location services enabled on your device or your home address confirmed in your account profile.

  1. Open the Brightline Home app and sign in. If you have trouble receiving your SMS verification code, see SMS Verification Code Not Arriving in Brightline Home.
  2. Tap My Project in the bottom navigation bar.
  3. On the Project Overview screen, locate the Site Survey milestone card and tap Schedule Now.
  4. Select your preferred date from the available calendar. Dates shown in green have open slots; dates in gray are fully booked.
  5. Choose a crew arrival window (morning: 8 AM–12 PM, or afternoon: 12 PM–4 PM). For more information about arrival windows, see What Is a Crew Arrival Window and How Long Is It?
  6. Confirm your site access details: gate codes, dogs on premises, or parking restrictions. Enter these in the Access Notes field (500-character limit).
  7. Tap Confirm Appointment. You will receive an email and push notification confirming your booking within 5 minutes.
  8. Add the appointment to your device calendar by tapping Add to Calendar on the confirmation screen.

Troubleshooting

The Schedule Now button is grayed out. Your agreement may still be in countersignature review, or your welcome call has not been logged as complete. Contact your project coordinator at the number shown in the My Team tab to resolve this before rescheduling.

No dates are available within 10 business days. High seasonal demand can temporarily reduce availability. Tap Notify Me on the calendar screen; the app will send a push notification within 24 hours when a closer slot opens due to a cancellation.

You do not receive a confirmation email within 5 minutes. Check your spam folder for messages from noreply@brightlinesolar.com. If the email has not arrived after 30 minutes, tap Resend Confirmation in My Project > Site Survey, or call support at 1-800-555-0192.
