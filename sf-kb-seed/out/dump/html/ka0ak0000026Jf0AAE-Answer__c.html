Common Reasons for Ground-Mount Permit Delays

Ground-mount permits involve more review departments than rooftop permits, which creates more opportunities for hold-ups. The most frequent causes are:

CauseTypical Added Delay
Jurisdiction requests a third-party structural peer review2–4 weeks
Zoning board or planning commission meeting cycle2–6 weeks
Missing or incorrect soil boring report1–3 weeks
HOA architectural review required1–4 weeks
Utility easement conflict flagged by county GIS1–3 weeks

How to Check Your Current Status

Open Brightline Home → My Project → Permits & Approvals. Each permit line item shows its current department, the date it was last updated, and any action items flagged with a yellow ! icon. If you see error code PMT-4402, the jurisdiction has placed the application on hold pending additional documentation — tap the error for details.

What Brightline Is Doing

Your project coordinator performs a status follow-up with the permitting office every 5 business days once a permit exceeds its estimated approval date. You will receive an in-app notification each time a follow-up is logged.

When to Contact Support

If your permit has exceeded the estimated date by more than 3 weeks and you have not received a status update in the last 7 days, contact Brightline Support via the app chat or call 1-800-555-0193. You may also want to review how to reschedule your final inspection after a weather delay if the permit delay has affected your inspection date.
