Why We Are Making This Change

Customer feedback collected between January and June 2026 showed that 61% of rapid shutdown alert recipients did not understand what action to take. The version 6.5 update directly addresses this by restructuring the alert experience from the ground up.

Key Improvements at a Glance

  • Plain-language titles: "Your solar array has safely shut down" instead of raw codes like RSD-02
  • Color-coded severity banners: Yellow (informational), Orange (action recommended), Red (contact support immediately)
  • Guided resolution steps: Tappable checklists inside each alert detail screen
  • Estimated restoration time: Displayed automatically for RSD-02 (grid-loss) events when utility outage data is available
  • False-alarm flag: One-tap reporting so our team can refine suppression logic
