Co-Borrower Eligibility

A co-borrower shares equal legal responsibility for your Brightline Solar loan. Unlike a cosigner, a co-borrower's name appears on both the financing agreement and the lien placed on your property, so eligibility requirements are strict.

Who Qualifies

  • Spouse or domestic partner — most common; must reside at or have a legal ownership interest in the installation address.
  • Co-owner of the property — any individual listed on the property deed qualifies automatically, provided they meet credit criteria.
  • Non-occupant co-owner — permitted in limited cases for properties held in joint tenancy or tenancy-in-common; subject to underwriter review.

Who Does Not Qualify

  • Relatives who have no ownership stake in the property
  • Business entities (LLCs, trusts, corporations) — these require a separate commercial financing pathway
  • Minors under 18

Required Documentation

DocumentAccepted Format
Government-issued photo IDDriver's license, passport, or state ID
Proof of property interestRecorded deed or title document
Social Security NumberProvided via secure Brightline e-consent form
Income verificationTwo most recent pay stubs or prior-year tax return

All documentation must be submitted through the Brightline Home app under Account → Financing → Manage Co-Borrower or via your assigned Brightline Financing Specialist. Processing takes 5–10 business days after a complete package is received.
