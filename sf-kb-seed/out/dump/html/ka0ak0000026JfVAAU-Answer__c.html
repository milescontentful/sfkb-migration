What Counts as a Partial Payment

A partial payment is any amount less than the full Statement Balance shown on your current invoice. Brightline Home accepts partial payments through the app, but your account will reflect an outstanding balance equal to the unpaid portion.

How the Remaining Balance Is Handled

The unpaid amount is carried forward to your next billing cycle and appears as a Previous Balance line item on your following invoice. You will not be charged a separate balance-transfer fee for this rollover.

Late Fees and Service Impacts

If your partial payment is received by the due date, the late-payment fee of $9.95 is waived for that cycle. However, if the total outstanding balance (current charges plus carried-over balance) remains unpaid past 30 days after the original due date, the $9.95 late fee is applied automatically. Repeated partial payments over three or more consecutive cycles may trigger an account review under Brightline's collections policy.

Monitoring Your Balance in Brightline Home

  1. Open Brightline Home and tap Billing in the bottom navigation bar.
  2. Select Account Summary to see your current balance, any carried-forward amount, and the next due date.
  3. Tap Make a Payment to submit an additional payment at any time before your next statement closes.

Financial Hardship Options

If partial payments are becoming a recurring necessity, you may qualify for the Brightline Hardship Program, which offers deferred payments and reduced minimums. See Brightline Hardship Program for eligibility details.

For questions about how failed or incomplete payments are retried, see Failed Payment Retry Schedule.
