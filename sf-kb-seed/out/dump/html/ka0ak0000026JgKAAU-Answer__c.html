Why Clear Roof Access Matters

Brightline technicians work at height with heavy panel assemblies and power tools. Any barrier that forces them to maneuver around or over unexpected objects increases the risk of injury, equipment damage, or a permit violation. If an obstruction cannot be cleared on the spot, the crew is required by Brightline's safety policy to stop work and reschedule — which can delay your system activation by 5–14 business days depending on crew availability.

Common Obstructions and How to Resolve Them

Obstruction TypeRequired Action
Locked side gate or fenceEnsure gate is unlocked or provide a key/code to your on-site contact
Overhanging tree branches within 3 ft of roof edgeTrim branches before appointment day
Satellite dishes or antennas in the work zoneContact provider to relocate, or note exception in the app
Stored items on a flat roof (furniture, planters, HVAC covers)Move items at least 5 ft from the marked work zone
Unsecured animals in the yardSecure pets indoors or in a contained area for the full appointment window

How to Check Your Work Zone

The marked work zone is shown in the Brightline Home app under My Appointments > Site Details > Roof Plan. If you believe something in your yard may be an issue but you're unsure, tap Flag a Concern on that screen to message your local crew at least 48 hours in advance.
