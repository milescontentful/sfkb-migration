Brightline Solar is pleased to announce a rate reduction across its SunPath Loan product lineup, effective July 1, 2026. The new rates apply to all loan agreements signed on or after that date and to eligible refinance requests submitted through the Brightline Home app by August 31, 2026.

Updated Rate Schedule

Loan TierPrevious APRNew APR (from July 1, 2026)
SunPath Standard (10-year)7.49%6.24%
SunPath Plus (15-year)7.99%6.99%
SunPath Premier (20-year)8.49%7.49%

Who Qualifies?

  • New customers signing a financing agreement on or after July 1, 2026
  • Existing customers in good standing who submit a refinance request in-app by August 31, 2026
  • Accounts with no missed payments in the prior 12 months

To view your current loan details and check refinance eligibility, open Brightline Home, tap Account → Financing → Loan Summary. For a full breakdown of what appears on your statement, see What information is included on my Brightline loan statement?
