Effective June 15, 2026, Brightline Home includes enhanced disclosure features for customers on the SunPath Flex variable-rate loan product. Variable rates on SunPath Flex loans are indexed to the Brightline Base Rate (BBR), which is reviewed quarterly in January, April, July, and October.

What's New in the App

  • Current Rate Banner — displayed at the top of Account → Financing → Loan Summary, showing your active APR as of the most recent review date
  • Rate Adjustment History — a scrollable table showing every rate change applied to your account over the past 24 months, including the effective date and the corresponding BBR index value
  • Next Review Date — a countdown indicator showing when your rate will next be evaluated
  • Rate Cap Reminder — a notice confirming your lifetime rate cap (typically BBR + 5.00%) as defined in your loan agreement
