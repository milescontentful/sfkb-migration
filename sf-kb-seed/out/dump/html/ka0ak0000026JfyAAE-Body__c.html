Virtual walkthroughs are available Monday through Saturday, 8:00 AM – 6:00 PM in your local time zone, with same-week slots often available. Availability may be limited in some regions during the initial rollout.

To select your format, open Brightline Home, navigate to My System > Appointments > Schedule Walkthrough, and choose Virtual or In-Person on the appointment type screen. If you opt for virtual, you will receive a secure video link via email and push notification 30 minutes before your session. After the walkthrough, your technician will send a summary checklist to your registered email within two business days. For help updating how you receive appointment notifications, see How to update your preferred contact method for installer communications.
