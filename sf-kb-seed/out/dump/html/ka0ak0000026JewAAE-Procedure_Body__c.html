Before you begin: A roof access waiver is only available to the primary account owner. Waivers must be submitted at least 24 hours before your appointment's arrival window opens; requests submitted later are subject to a late-cancellation fee of $75. Acceptable waiver reasons include active roof repair by a licensed contractor, a court or HOA-issued roof restriction order, or a documented structural safety concern. You will need to upload supporting documentation (PDF, JPG, or PNG, max 10 MB per file).

  1. Open the Brightline Home app and tap Service, then Upcoming Appointments.
  2. Select the appointment for which you need the waiver and tap Manage Appointment.
  3. Tap Request Roof Access Waiver. If this option does not appear, your appointment type does not require roof access and no waiver is needed.
  4. Select the reason that best describes your situation from the dropdown: Active Third-Party Roof Work, HOA or Legal Restriction, or Structural Safety Concern.
  5. Tap Upload Documentation and attach your supporting file(s). At least one document is required for the waiver to be reviewed.
  6. In the Additional Notes field, briefly describe the situation and your earliest available date for rescheduling.
  7. Tap Submit Waiver Request. You will receive an email acknowledgment within 15 minutes. A Brightline Scheduling specialist will review your request and respond within 1 business day with a waiver decision and a rescheduled appointment offer.

Troubleshooting

I do not see the Request Roof Access Waiver option. This option only appears for appointments classified as requiring physical roof access (for example, panel cleaning, inverter swap, or post-storm inspection). If your appointment is an indoor electrical inspection, roof access is not required and no waiver is necessary.

My waiver was denied. Waivers are denied when the submitted documentation does not match the selected reason or is illegible. You will receive an email with the specific denial reason and instructions to resubmit. You have 4 hours from the denial email to resubmit before the original appointment is cancelled and the $75 fee applies.

I submitted a waiver but my appointment still shows as Confirmed. Appointment status updates after the waiver is approved, not at submission. This is expected. See Can I change my crew arrival window after scheduling? for related scheduling options while your waiver is under review.
