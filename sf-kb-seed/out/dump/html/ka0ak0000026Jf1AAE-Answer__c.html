Ground-Mount Permit Document Checklist

Ground-mount installations require a more extensive permit package than rooftop systems because they involve foundation work, setback reviews, and sometimes stormwater or grading approvals. Brightline prepares and submits the following on your behalf:

  • Structural engineering report – stamped by a licensed engineer, covering the racking foundation (helical piers, ballast, or concrete footings depending on your soil assessment)
  • Electrical single-line diagram – showing the AC/DC wiring path from array to your main panel or battery system
  • Site plan – scaled plot map indicating array location, property lines, setbacks, and overhead utility lines
  • Geotechnical or soil report – required by most jurisdictions when helical piers or concrete footings are used
  • Zoning compliance letter – confirming the array meets local height and setback rules

What You May Need to Provide

In some counties, the permit authority requires the homeowner of record to sign a notarized authorization form before Brightline can submit. Your project coordinator will send this via DocuSign if it applies to your jurisdiction. You may also need to provide a copy of your current property survey if one was not completed during your site assessment.

Typical Review Timeline

Once your complete document package is submitted, most jurisdictions return a decision within 15–35 business days. Complex parcels with HOA overlay districts or agricultural zoning can take up to 60 business days. You can track permit status in the Brightline Home app under My Project → Permits → Ground-Mount Permit.
