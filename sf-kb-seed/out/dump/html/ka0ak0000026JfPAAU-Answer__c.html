Where to Find Your Payment Receipts

Every payment processed on your Brightline account automatically generates a receipt, which is stored in the Brightline Home app and emailed to your account address within 15 minutes of the payment clearing.

Viewing Receipts in the App

  1. Open Brightline Home and tap Account in the bottom navigation bar.
  2. Select Billing & Payments, then tap Payment History.
  3. Locate the payment you need and tap it to open the detail view.
  4. Tap Download Receipt (PDF) to save a copy to your device, or tap Email Receipt to resend it to your address on file.

Resending a Receipt by Email

If you did not receive the original receipt email, check your spam folder first. If it is not there, use the Email Receipt option described above. The email will arrive from receipts@mail.brightlinesolar.com within 5 minutes.

Receipts for Check Payments

Mailed check payments take additional time to post. A receipt will appear in Payment History within 5–7 business days of Brightline receiving your check. See check payment processing time for details on posting timelines.

Receipt Not Appearing?

If a receipt is missing after the expected window, contact Brightline Support at 1-800-555-0192 or use in-app chat. Have your payment confirmation number ready (format: PAY-XXXXXXXX).
