What to Expect

When your wave begins, you will see an MFA setup prompt immediately after entering your password. You have 48 hours to complete setup. If setup is not completed within that window, your account will be locked with error code AUTH_MFA_REQUIRED_7 until MFA enrollment is finished.

How to Unlock

  1. Go to brightlinehome.app/unlock or open the Brightline Home app.
  2. Enter your email address and select Set Up MFA to Unlock.
  3. Choose SMS or authenticator app as your second factor and complete verification.

Partner API integrations are not subject to MFA enforcement but should ensure OAuth2 flows remain valid. See how to resolve OAuth2 token errors in the Partner API for guidance. Contact support at 1-800-555-0194 with questions.
