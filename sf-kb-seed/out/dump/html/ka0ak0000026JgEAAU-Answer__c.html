Rescheduling Your Site Survey in Brightline Home

To reschedule, open Brightline Home and navigate to My Project > Appointments > Upcoming. Tap your site survey card and select Reschedule. You will see a live calendar showing the next available slots in your area — select a new date and time, then tap Confirm Reschedule. A confirmation email and push notification are sent immediately.

Cancellation Policy

  • More than 24 hours before the appointment: Cancellation is free. You may cancel directly in the app via My Project > Appointments > Cancel Visit.
  • Within 24 hours of the appointment: A $75 late-cancellation fee is applied to your account. This fee appears on your next invoice as a line item labeled Late Survey Cancellation Fee.
  • No-show (technician arrives, no one home): A $125 no-show fee is charged, and your project timeline may be extended by up to 10 business days depending on technician availability in your region.

Rescheduling by Phone

You may also contact your assigned installer directly. To find their contact details, go to My Project > Installer Info in Brightline Home. For guidance on locating this screen, see How to view your installer contact information in Brightline Home.

How Many Times Can I Reschedule?

Brightline allows up to 2 reschedules per site survey at no charge (subject to the 24-hour policy above). A third reschedule request requires written approval from your project coordinator and may delay your estimated installation date. If you need to reschedule due to a weather event, no cancellation fee is assessed — see Will I be charged a fee if my appointment is cancelled due to weather? for details.
