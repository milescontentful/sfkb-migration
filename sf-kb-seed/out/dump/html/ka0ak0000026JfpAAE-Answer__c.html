Why Weather Delays Happen

Brightline Solar crews work at height on rooftops and with high-voltage electrical components. To protect both our technicians and your property, we follow strict safety thresholds. An appointment will be automatically postponed if any of the following conditions are forecast or active within a 4-hour window around your scheduled start time:

  • Sustained winds above 25 mph or gusts above 35 mph
  • Active precipitation (rain, sleet, hail, or snow)
  • Lightning within a 10-mile radius
  • Temperatures below 20 °F or heat index above 105 °F

How You Are Notified

When a weather delay is triggered, you will receive a push notification through the Brightline Home app and an email to your account address no later than 5:00 AM local time on the day of your appointment. The notification will include a new proposed date, typically within 5–10 business days, depending on crew availability in your region.

What Happens Next

Your rescheduled date is confirmed automatically—no action is required on your part unless the new time does not work for you. To request a different date, open the app and navigate to My Projects > Appointments > Reschedule. There is no rescheduling fee for weather-related delays. For tips on getting your home ready once the new date is set, see How to Prepare Your Home for Inspection Day.
