Common Reasons for an Unexpected Balance

Several factors can cause your closing balance to differ from what you anticipated. The most frequent reasons are listed below.

1. Interest Accrues Daily

Brightline Solar loans use simple daily interest. This means interest accumulates each day based on your current principal. If your payment arrived even a few days late, additional interest will have accrued before your payment was applied, leaving a slightly higher balance than expected.

2. Payment Application Order

Payments are applied in the following order: (1) outstanding fees, (2) accrued interest, (3) principal. If you carry a late fee or returned-payment fee from a prior period, your payment reduces those charges first, and less principal is paid down than you may have anticipated.

3. Partial Payments

If you made a partial payment in the previous period, the unpaid remainder rolled forward and continued to accrue interest. See How does Brightline apply partial payments to my account balance? for details on how this works.

4. Hardship Deferral Adjustments

If you enrolled in a payment deferral through the Brightline Hardship Program, deferred amounts and any associated interest adjustments will appear as separate line items on your statement. Refer to Hardship Program Expansion: Extended Deferral (January 2026) for program terms.
