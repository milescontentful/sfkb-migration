1. Create a job: 
    POST /v2/exports
{ "site_ids": ["..."], "from": "2025-01-01", "to": "2025-12-31", "granularity": "hourly" }
  2. Poll GET /v2/exports/{id} until status is ready. Typical wait: 30–120 seconds.
  3. Download download_url (a signed URL valid for 1 hour). Files are gzipped CSV.

Jobs are limited to 500 sites and 3 years each. Export jobs do not count against the per-request rate limit.
