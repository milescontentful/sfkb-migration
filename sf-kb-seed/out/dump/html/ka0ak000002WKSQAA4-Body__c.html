Brightline Solar launched the Weather Delay Tracker inside the Brightline Home app on March 3, 2025. The tracker is visible to any customer whose installation appointment falls within the next 14 calendar days.

Where to find it

Open Brightline Home → tap My Project → select Appointment Details → scroll to the Weather Status card.

Status indicators

StatusMeaning
On ScheduleNo weather concerns detected for your window
WatchBorderline conditions; monitoring hourly
DelayedAppointment cancelled; reschedule offer pending
RescheduledNew date confirmed; tap to accept

Customers have 24 hours to accept a rescheduled date through the app before the slot is released. If no action is taken, the system will auto-assign the next available date and send a confirmation. A reschedule offer declined twice within the same delay event will require a manual call to the Scheduling team (reference code WD-MANUAL).

For same-day arrival window communications related to your rescheduled appointment, see Same-Day Crew Arrival Window Updates.
