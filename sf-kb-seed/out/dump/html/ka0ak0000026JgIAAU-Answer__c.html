What Happens When Roof Access Is Blocked

If a Brightline technician arrives at your property and cannot safely or legally access the roof, the visit is logged as an Access Failure (AF) event. The technician will wait up to 20 minutes for the issue to be resolved before departing.

Fees That May Apply

ScenarioFee
First AF event on your account (lifetime)Waived
Second or subsequent AF event$95 return-trip fee
AF caused by locked gate with no contact response$95 return-trip fee
AF caused by confirmed weather or utility hazardWaived — see weather delay policy

How to Rebook

  1. Open Brightline Home and go to Schedule > My Appointments.
  2. Tap the affected appointment and select Rebook After Access Failure.
  3. Choose a new date — priority rebooking slots (within 5 business days) are offered at no extra cost for first-time AF events.

Disputing an AF Fee

If you believe a return-trip fee was applied in error — for example, because the technician arrived outside your confirmed window — you can dispute the charge directly in the app under Billing > Dispute a Charge.
