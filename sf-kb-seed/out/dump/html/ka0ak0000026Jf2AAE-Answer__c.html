Why Changes After Submission Are Complicated

Once Brightline submits your ground-mount permit package to the Authority Having Jurisdiction (AHJ), any change to the system design — panel count, inverter model, array orientation, or foundation type — typically requires a permit amendment or a full resubmission. Ground-mount systems are especially sensitive to design changes because the structural engineering report, site plan, and setback calculations are all tied to the exact array footprint and weight load.

Common Changes and Their Impact

Change TypeLikely OutcomeAdded Wait Time
Adding 1–4 panels (same footprint)Amendment required5–15 business days
Expanding array footprintNew structural stamp + resubmission15–30 business days
Swapping inverter modelElectrical diagram update, amendment5–10 business days
Changing foundation typeFull resubmission likely20–40 business days

Amendment Fees

Brightline charges a $175 permit amendment processing fee for changes requested after initial submission. Some jurisdictions also charge their own amendment filing fee, which is passed through at cost. Both charges will appear as a line item in your Brightline Home billing summary.

How to Request a Change

  1. Open Brightline Home and go to My Project → Design.
  2. Tap Request a Design Change and describe the modification.
  3. Your project coordinator will assess permit impact within 2 business days and send a written impact summary before any amendment is filed.
