Enabling Paperless Loan Statement Delivery

By default, Brightline Home stores your statements in the app but does not automatically email them. You can enable automatic email delivery—sometimes called e-Statement enrollment—in a few steps.

Steps in the Brightline Home App

  1. Open Brightline Home and tap Account in the bottom navigation bar.
  2. Select Financing, then tap Statement Preferences.
  3. Under Delivery Method, switch the toggle from App Only to Email + App.
  4. Confirm or update the email address shown. You may add a second address (e.g., a co-borrower's email) by tapping + Add Recipient.
  5. Tap Save Preferences. You will receive a confirmation email within 15 minutes.

When Will the Change Take Effect?

Your preference update applies to the next statement cycle. If your current cycle closes within 48 hours of making the change, the very next statement may still be app-only; the one after that will be emailed.

Troubleshooting Delivery Issues

  • Check your spam or junk folder for messages from statements@mail.brightlinesolar.com.
  • If you are not receiving login emails either, review the steps at SMS verification code not arriving in Brightline Home as carrier filtering can sometimes affect other transactional emails too.
  • To download a past statement manually while you wait, follow the instructions in How do I view or download my loan statement in Brightline Home?

Opting Back Out

Return to Account → Financing → Statement Preferences and switch the toggle back to App Only at any time. There is no fee to change your delivery preference.
