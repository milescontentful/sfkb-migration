Preparing Roof Access Before Your Appointment

Proper roof access preparation helps your technician start work on time and avoids same-day rescheduling fees. Please complete the following steps at least 24 hours before your scheduled appointment window.

Clear the Surrounding Area

  • Remove vehicles, trailers, or equipment parked within 10 feet of the roofline on all sides where work is planned.
  • Trim or tie back overhanging tree branches that could block ladder placement or panel positioning.
  • Ensure ground surfaces near the home are stable — soft or uneven soil must be compacted or have boards placed for ladder feet.

Gate and Yard Access

  • Unlock all gates leading to the side or rear of the property by 7:00 AM on appointment day.
  • Secure pets indoors or in a fenced area separate from the work zone.
  • If your HOA requires a visitor pass or escort, arrange this in advance and notify your Brightline coordinator by calling 1-800-BRTLINE (1-800-278-5463).

Roof Condition Notes

If you are aware of any existing roof damage, soft spots, or recent repairs, disclose these in the Brightline Home app under My Home > Property Details > Roof Notes before the visit. Undisclosed hazards may require the technician to pause work and could result in a return-trip charge.
