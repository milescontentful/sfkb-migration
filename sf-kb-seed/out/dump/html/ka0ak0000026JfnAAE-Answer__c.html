Managing Your Scheduled Reports

Yes — you can edit, pause, or delete any scheduled report at any time without losing your historical delivery records. Follow the steps below based on what you need to do.

How to Edit a Scheduled Report

  1. Open Brightline Home and tap Reports in the bottom navigation bar.
  2. Select the Scheduled tab at the top of the Reports screen.
  3. Tap the report you want to change, then tap Edit Schedule.
  4. Update any combination of the following: delivery frequency (Daily, Weekly, Monthly), report type (Production, Savings, Battery, or Combined), recipient email addresses, and preferred delivery time (in your local timezone).
  5. Tap Save Changes. The updated schedule takes effect on the next scheduled run.

How to Pause a Scheduled Report

If you want to stop deliveries temporarily without deleting the schedule, toggle the Active switch to Off on the report detail screen. A paused report retains all its settings. To resume, toggle the switch back to On — delivery will resume at the next scheduled interval.

How to Delete a Scheduled Report

Tap Edit Schedule, then scroll to the bottom and tap Delete Schedule. Confirm the deletion in the dialog that appears. This action is permanent and cannot be undone, though past delivered reports remain accessible under Reports > History.

For guidance on first creating a scheduled report, see How do I schedule a recurring production report in Brightline Home?. If your reports include weather-adjusted figures, see Weather Adjustments Now Applied to Production Reports for details on what those numbers mean.
