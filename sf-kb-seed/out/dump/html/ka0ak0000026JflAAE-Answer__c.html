Setting Up a Recurring Report Schedule

Brightline Home lets you schedule automatic production reports so your data arrives on a cadence that works for you. Follow these steps to configure a recurring report:

  1. Open Brightline Home and tap the Reports tab in the bottom navigation bar.
  2. Tap Schedule a Report in the upper-right corner.
  3. Under Report Type, choose one of the following: Daily Summary, Weekly Rollup, or Monthly Statement.
  4. Set your preferred Delivery Day & Time. Weekly reports default to Monday at 7:00 AM local time; monthly reports default to the 1st of the month.
  5. Under Delivery Method, select Email, In-App Notification, or Both.
  6. Optionally enable Include Weather Adjustments to add weather-corrected figures to each report (see Weather Adjustments Now Applied to Production Reports).
  7. Tap Save Schedule. You will see a confirmation banner: "Your report schedule has been saved and will begin on your next scheduled date."

Editing or Canceling a Schedule

To modify an existing schedule, go to Reports > My Schedules, tap the schedule you want to change, and select Edit or Delete Schedule. Changes take effect immediately and apply starting with the next scheduled delivery.

Limits & Notes

  • You may have up to 5 active report schedules per account.
  • Reports are generated using data finalized by 2:00 AM local time, so same-day data may be incomplete in Daily Summary reports delivered before that cutoff.
  • For lifetime production figures included in reports, see How to View Your Lifetime Production Stats in Brightline Home.
