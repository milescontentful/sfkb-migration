Before you begin: You must have a site survey already scheduled on your account. Have your new preferred date ready before starting. Note the following policy limits: rescheduling is free if completed more than 48 hours before your appointment start time. Rescheduling or cancelling within 48 hours incurs a $75 late-change fee charged to your payment method on file. Same-day cancellations (within 4 hours of the window start) incur a $150 fee. If you need to update your payment card before proceeding, see How Do I Update an Expiring or Expired Payment Card in Brightline Home.

  1. Open the Brightline Home app and sign in.
  2. Tap My Project in the bottom navigation bar.
  3. On the Project Overview screen, tap the Site Survey milestone card to expand it.
  4. Tap Manage Appointment in the upper-right corner of the card.
  5. Choose either Reschedule or Cancel.
    • Reschedule: Select a new date and arrival window, update any access notes if needed, then tap Confirm New Appointment. A revised confirmation email is sent within 5 minutes.
    • Cancel: Select a cancellation reason from the dropdown (required), then tap Confirm Cancellation. Your project timeline will pause until a new survey is scheduled.
  6. If a fee applies, a modal will display the charge amount and your card ending in the last four digits shown. Tap Accept & Continue to authorize the fee, or Go Back to keep your original appointment.
  7. Confirm the action. You will receive an updated email and push notification within 5 minutes.

Troubleshooting

The Manage Appointment button does not appear. Your survey may have been locked by your project coordinator because crew routing has already been finalized. In this case, call support at 1-800-555-0192 at least 24 hours before your appointment; an agent can make changes manually and advise on any applicable fees.

You were charged a fee but believe it was applied in error. If you rescheduled more than 48 hours before your appointment and were still charged $75, tap My Project > Billing History to locate the transaction, then tap Dispute Charge. Include your appointment confirmation number (format: SS-XXXXXXXX) in the dispute form. Resolution typically takes 3–5 business days. For guidance on what happens after a weather event forces a change outside your control, see What to Do When Your Installation Is Delayed by Weather.

Your cancellation confirmation email did not arrive. Check spam for noreply@brightlinesolar.com. If absent after 30 minutes, verify the cancellation status under My Project > Site Survey; if the appointment still shows as active, the cancellation did not save—repeat the steps above or call support.
