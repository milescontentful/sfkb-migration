Short answer

Your invoice has two parts: the fixed financing payment (which never changes) and the utility true-up for any power you drew from the grid. The second part is what moves.

Check these first

  1. Production vs. estimate. Open Brightline Home → Reports → Monthly Production and compare to the proposal. Anything below 85% of estimate for two months in a row should be reported as low production.
  2. Usage increase. A new EV charger, heat pump, or pool pump can add 300–600 kWh/month.
  3. Utility rate change. Most utilities adjusted time-of-use rates in the last 12 months. Your true-up is billed at the utility's current rate, not the rate in the proposal.
  4. Shading or soiling. See cleaning your panels.
