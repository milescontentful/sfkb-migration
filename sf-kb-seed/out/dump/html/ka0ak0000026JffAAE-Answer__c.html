Supported Languages

Brightline Home currently supports the following display languages: English (US), Spanish (ES), French (FR), and Portuguese (BR). All app screens, alerts, billing summaries, and system status messages are fully translated in each language.

How to Change Your Language

  1. Open the Brightline Home app and sign in.
  2. Tap your profile avatar in the top-right corner of the dashboard.
  3. Select Account Settings from the dropdown menu.
  4. Tap Preferences, then tap Language & Region.
  5. Choose your preferred language from the list and tap Save.

The app will reload automatically within 2–3 seconds and display all content in the selected language. No reinstall is required.

Notes & Limitations

  • Language changes apply only to the app interface. Formal documents such as loan statements, invoices, and permit-related records are issued in English only.
  • Push notification language updates may take up to 15 minutes to take effect after saving.
  • If you do not see your preferred language in the list, it is not yet supported. You can submit a language request via Help & Support → Feature Request inside the app.

For help understanding alerts you may see in the app, see Temperature Warning Alerts Now Available in Brightline Home.
