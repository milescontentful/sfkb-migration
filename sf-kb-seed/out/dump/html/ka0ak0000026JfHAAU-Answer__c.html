How to Submit an Invoice Dispute

If a line item on your invoice looks incorrect, you can open a formal dispute within 60 days of the invoice date. Disputes submitted after this window may not be eligible for review.

Option 1: Dispute via Brightline Home App

  1. Open the Brightline Home app and tap Billing in the bottom navigation bar.
  2. Select the invoice you want to dispute, then tap View Invoice Details.
  3. Tap the Dispute a Charge button (flag icon) next to the line item in question.
  4. Choose a dispute reason from the dropdown (e.g., Charge not recognized, Incorrect amount, Already paid, or Other).
  5. Add any supporting notes, then tap Submit Dispute. You will receive a confirmation email with a dispute reference number (format: DSP-XXXXXXXX).

Option 2: Call Customer Support

Call 1-800-555-0192 (Mon–Fri, 7 a.m.–7 p.m. local time) and have your account number and invoice number ready. An agent will log the dispute on your behalf.

What Happens Next?

  • Our billing team reviews disputes within 10 business days.
  • You will receive an email update at each stage: Under Review, Decision Pending, and Resolved.
  • If the dispute is upheld, a credit or refund will be applied within 5–7 business days of resolution.
  • Your account will not be sent to collections for the disputed amount while the case is open.

If your dispute involves a prorated charge you believe was calculated incorrectly, see Can I get a refund or credit for a prorated charge I was billed incorrectly?
