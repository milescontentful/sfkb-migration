Effective date: November 1, 2025

When a scheduled payment fails, Brightline's billing system generates error code PAY-4010 and immediately queues the account for the updated retry sequence below.

  • Day 0: Initial charge attempt fails; email and push notification sent.
  • Day 2: First automatic retry.
  • Day 5: Second automatic retry; in-app banner displayed in Brightline Home.
  • Day 9: Third automatic retry; SMS reminder sent.
  • Day 14: Fourth and final retry. If unsuccessful, a $15 late fee is added and the account enters manual review.

You can update your payment method at any time by opening Brightline Home and navigating to Account → Billing → Payment Methods → Add or Replace. Updating before the Day 14 retry will clear the pending PAY-4010 flag automatically.

Customers experiencing financial hardship may qualify for payment deferral. See Brightline Hardship Program Launch 2025 for eligibility details.

No action is required if your payment succeeds on any retry attempt; the late fee will not be applied.
