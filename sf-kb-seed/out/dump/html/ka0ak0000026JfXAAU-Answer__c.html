Accessing Your Loan Statement

Your monthly loan statement is available in the Brightline Home app within 24–48 hours after your statement period closes. To find it, follow these steps:

  1. Open the Brightline Home app and tap Account in the bottom navigation bar.
  2. Select Financing, then tap Loan Statements.
  3. You will see a list of statements sorted by date, labeled with the statement period (e.g., May 1 – May 31, 2026).
  4. Tap any statement to open a detailed view, or tap the Download PDF button to save a copy to your device.

What Is Included in a Statement

Each loan statement contains the following information:

  • Opening balance – the amount owed at the start of the period
  • Payments received – all payments applied during the statement period, including partial payments
  • Interest accrued – interest charged under your loan agreement (typically at your fixed APR)
  • Fees assessed – any late fees (standard fee: $15.00) or returned payment fees ($25.00)
  • Closing balance – your remaining principal balance
  • Next payment due date and amount

Statement Delivery by Email

If you prefer to receive statements by email automatically each month, go to Account > Notification Preferences > Financing and enable Monthly Loan Statement Email. Statements are sent as PDF attachments to your address on file.
