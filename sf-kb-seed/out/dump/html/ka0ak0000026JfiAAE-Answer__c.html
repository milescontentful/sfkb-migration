App Language vs. Notification Language

Yes — Brightline Home keeps your app display language and your notification language as two independent settings. Changing one does not automatically change the other.

How to Set Your Notification Language

Follow these steps to update the language used for email and SMS notifications:

  1. Open Brightline Home and go to Profile → Notifications → Notification Preferences.
  2. Tap Notification Language at the top of the screen.
  3. Select your preferred language from the same list of six supported languages.
  4. Tap Save Changes.

Changes take effect for all notifications sent after you save. Notifications already queued (within the next 15 minutes) may still arrive in the previous language.

Which Notifications Are Affected

Notification TypeSupports Language Setting
Production alertsYes
Billing & payment remindersYes
Temperature warning alertsYes
Outage & system status updatesYes
Legal notices & loan disclosuresNo — English only

Tip

If you want everything in the same language, set both Display Language and Notification Language to match. For more on temperature alert notifications specifically, see Temperature Warning Alerts Now Available in Brightline Home.
